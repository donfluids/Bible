**Description**

```
Read the King James Version, the Berean Standard Bible and the World English Bible, and study the Hebrew and Greek words behind them.

• The KJV, the Berean Standard Bible (a modern translation) and the World English Bible, switched with one tap.
• Tap an underlined word in the KJV or the BSB for the Hebrew or Greek word behind it: the form in that verse with its grammar in plain words, a short modern meaning, pronunciation, a fuller entry from Abbott-Smith's Greek lexicon or the Brown–Driver–Briggs outline for Hebrew, and Strong's dictionary entry.
• Every verse where that Hebrew or Greek word stands, with how each translation renders it ("God 2,075 · gods 190 · judges 3"); tap a rendering to see only those verses.
• Hebrew and Greek words: the original text of every verse, word by word, with transliteration and a short meaning. Greek words missing from the modern critical editions are marked.
• The translators' footnotes and cross references, as small letters in the text.
• Search the text, the dictionary ("love", "logos", or a Hebrew or Greek word), a Strong's number such as G26, or type a reference such as John 3:16 or Ps 23 to go straight there.
• An introduction to each book: who wrote it, when and for whom, its themes, an outline and key verses.
• Cross references for every verse, and parallel passages where the same event or words are told again (the Gospels, Kings and Chronicles).
• Compare a verse in the KJV, the BSB and the WEB side by side with the Hebrew or Greek.
• Highlight a whole verse, a few words, or a passage running over several verses, in four colours. Keep bookmarks and notes.
• Notebook: write sermon notes or a study. A reference such as John 3:16 becomes a link to the verse, with its text shown beneath. Add a verse to a page straight from the reader, and share a page with its verses.
• Listen to a chapter read aloud by your phone's own voice, with the verse being read marked as it goes.
• Share a verse as a picture card, for a birthday, a wedding, Christmas or any day, with your own message.
• Light, sepia and dark themes. Pinch to change the text size. Read verse by verse or in paragraphs.

No internet needed: the whole text is inside the app. No ads, no account, and no data collected. Your highlights and notes stay on your phone, and in its iCloud backup if you use one.

Sources: King James Version 1769 and World English Bible, from eBible.org (public domain; the KJV outside the United Kingdom); Berean Standard Bible (public domain), via CrossWire; Hebrew and Greek text and meanings from STEPBible.org, Tyndale House Cambridge (CC BY 4.0); Strong's dictionary from Open Scriptures (CC BY-SA); Abbott-Smith's Greek lexicon (public domain); the Brown–Driver–Briggs outline from Open Scriptures (CC BY 4.0); cross references from OpenBible.info (CC BY 4.0); parallel passages from the UBS Parallel Passages Database, © 2023 United Bible Societies (CC BY-SA 4.0). Details are under Sources and licences in the app.
```
