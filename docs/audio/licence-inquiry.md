# Asking about the Malayalam audio on eBible.org

The recording at https://ebible.org/mal2015/mp3/ (New Testament, Psalms and Proverbs of the
1910 Malayalam Bible in today's spelling) is the quality wanted for the app. Its page says
"copyright © 2015 The Free Bible Foundation CC-BY-SA" but names no narrator or studio and
does not say that the licence covers the audio. Before any of it goes into an app, that
needs an answer in writing.

Where to send it:

- The Free Bible Foundation: https://tfbf.in (its contact page or address).
- eBible.org: the form at https://ebible.org/contact.php, reason "Publishing or copyright
  question", translation "mal2015".

Send from a riversresearch.org address, and keep the reply.

## The message

Subject: Malayalam 1910 audio (mal2015) — licence and source of the recording

Hello,

I am writing from Rivers Research LLC. We publish a free Malayalam and English Bible app
with no advertising, which uses the 1910 Malayalam Bible in contemporary orthography. We
would like to offer the audio recording published at https://ebible.org/mal2015/mp3/ to
our readers, and want to be sure we do so correctly.

Could you tell us:

1. Who made the recording (narrator, studio or ministry), and in what year?
2. Who holds the rights in the audio? Is it The Free Bible Foundation?
3. Is the audio itself licensed under Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0, as the
   text is? If not, under what terms may it be redistributed?
4. Who holds the rights in the background music, and does the same licence cover it?
5. May the files be re-encoded at a lower bitrate for mobile download, with the content
   otherwise unchanged?
6. How would you like the recording to be credited?
7. Is a recording of the rest of the Old Testament available, or planned?

We would credit the recording and its licence on the app's Sources screen, and offer the
files as an optional download, unchanged apart from the re-encoding.

Thank you for making this recording available.
