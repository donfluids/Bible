# Malayalam meanings for common Hebrew and Greek words: for checking

In the interlinear, these words show the Malayalam below in place of the English. They are words the Malayalam Bibles seldom give a word of their own: Malayalam says them with an ending, written here with a hyphen (-ൽ means "in", added to a word), or leaves them out. An empty meaning (—) shows nothing, as for "the", which Malayalam does not have.

Together these words are about a quarter of all the words in the Bible, so a wrong one shows on most pages. Please correct any you would put differently; the list is `src/data/interlinear-ml.json`.
