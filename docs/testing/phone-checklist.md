# Phone test before a release

About 20 minutes a phone. Do it on as many of these as you can borrow:

- an old or cheap Android (Android 7 to 9, 2 or 3 GB of memory);
- a Samsung phone;
- a recent phone (Pixel or similar);
- a tablet, and a Chromebook with a keyboard, if you have them.

Write the phone's name and Android version at the top of your notes. For each line,
note ✔ or what went wrong, with a screenshot. Write down the version shown at the
foot of Settings (for example "0.4.15 (42)") so the notes say which build.

## 1. Install and update

- [ ] **Update over the old version** first (from Play, or install the new APK over
      the old one). Before updating, make a highlight, a verse note, a bookmark and a
      notebook page. After updating, all four are still there.
- [ ] The first start after the update shows the Bible within a few seconds (it may
      say it is preparing the text once).
- [ ] **Fresh install** on a second phone, or after uninstalling: "Preparing the Bible
      text…" for a few seconds, then John 1 with the tip about tapping words.

## 2. Reading

- [ ] Swipe or use the arrows to the next and previous chapter; the book title in the
      top bar opens the book list, and a book and chapter open.
- [ ] Scroll down: the bars fold away; scroll up: they come back.
- [ ] Switch the Bible (the ഇംഗ്ലീഷ് / KJV button at the bottom) and back.
- [ ] Turn on Greek/Hebrew at the bottom: words appear under each verse.
- [ ] Turn the phone sideways: the text and bars fit, nothing is cut off. On the
      Malayalam app, Malayalam and English stand side by side.

## 3. Words and verses

- [ ] Tap an underlined word: the sheet shows the Greek or Hebrew word, its meaning
      in Malayalam (or English) and "See all verses". The speaker button says the word
      (it may need a Greek or Hebrew voice).
- [ ] Hold a verse: the menu opens. Try copy, share, bookmark, a highlight colour, a
      note, "Mark text" (drag over a few words), Compare.
- [ ] Add a verse to the notebook from that menu.

## 4. Listen (read aloud)

- [ ] Tap the speaker in the top bar: the chapter is read in the phone's voice, the
      verse being read is shown, and it goes on to the next chapter.
- [ ] Pause, play, next and previous verse, speed.
- [ ] Lock the screen while it reads: note whether it carries on.
- [ ] Have someone call you while it reads: it stops or pauses, and you can carry on
      afterwards.
- [ ] Malayalam app: if the phone has no Malayalam voice, the message explains what to
      install.

## 5. Cards

- [ ] Hold a verse → Share as card. Pick an occasion, a design, square and tall.
- [ ] **Your photo**: the phone's own photo picker opens (the app asks for no photo
      permission). Pick a photo, crop it, try Light and Dark, Faint to Strong, Blur,
      Top/Middle/Bottom, Book/Plain, Pictures and lines.
- [ ] Share picture to WhatsApp (or another app): the picture is sharp and looks like
      the preview.
- [ ] Save as my design. Close the app completely (swipe it away), open it again,
      open a card: the saved design is still first in the designs, with its photo.
- [ ] Long-press the saved design: it can be removed.

## 6. Notebook, Saved, Search

- [ ] New page in the notebook: type a title and "John 3:16" (or യോഹന്നാൻ 3:16); after
      Done the reference is a link and the verse is quoted under it. Edit and share
      the page; delete a page.
- [ ] Saved shows the bookmarks, highlights and notes from section 3.
- [ ] Search for a word: results start in the New Testament; the Old and New Testament
      and book buttons narrow the list; Show more adds verses. Search for a reference
      (John 3:16) and a number (G26).

## 7. Settings and display

- [ ] Each theme (Light, Sepia, Dark, follow the phone); Fluorescent highlights in dark.
- [ ] **With the app open on a chapter**, switch the phone's own dark mode on and off
      (quick settings): the whole screen changes, the title included.
- [ ] Largest text size in the app, and the phone's largest font (Android Settings →
      Display → Font size): nothing overlaps or is cut off in the reader, the word
      sheet, the verse menu and Settings.
- [ ] Interface language English/Malayalam (or Tamil) switches every screen.

## 8. Accessibility

- [ ] Turn on TalkBack (Android Settings → Accessibility). Every button says what it
      does (in the interface language), a verse can be read, a tab can be changed.

## 9. Tablet and Chromebook

- [ ] A tablet on its side: tapping a word opens the panel at the right and the text
      stays in view; another word can be tapped with the panel open.
- [ ] Chromebook keyboard: ← and → turn the chapter, Ctrl+F opens search, Esc closes a
      sheet or goes back.

## 10. Odd cases

- [ ] Phone nearly full (under 500 MB free): the app still opens; a fresh install says
      if there is not room to prepare the text.
- [ ] Leave the app for a few hours (or open many other apps), come back: it opens at
      the same place.
- [ ] Back button / back gesture closes a sheet first, then goes back a screen, and
      leaves the app from the reader.

When something is wrong, send the notes and screenshots; say what you did just
before it happened.
