# Phone test before a release

About 20 minutes a phone. Do it on as many of these as you can borrow:

- an old or cheap Android (Android 7 to 9, 2 or 3 GB of memory);
- a Samsung phone;
- a recent phone (Pixel or similar);
- a tablet, and a Chromebook with a keyboard, if you have them.

Write the phone's name and Android version at the top of your notes. For each line,
note ✔ or what went wrong, with a screenshot. Write down the version shown at the
foot of Settings (for example "0.4.15 (42)") so the notes say which build.

## 1. Install and update

- [ ] **Update over the old version** first (from Play, or install the new APK over
      the old one). Before updating, make a highlight, a verse note, a bookmark and a
      notebook page. After updating, all four are still there.
- [ ] The first start after the update shows the Bible within a few seconds (it may
      say it is preparing the text once).
- [ ] **Fresh install** on a second phone, or after uninstalling: "Preparing the Bible
      text…" for a few seconds, then John 1 with the tip about tapping words.

## 2. Reading

- [ ] Swipe or use the arrows to the next and previous chapter; the book title in the
      top bar opens the book list, and a book and chapter open.
- [ ] Scroll down: the bars fold away; scroll up: they come back.
- [ ] Switch the Bible (the ഇംഗ്ലീഷ് / KJV button at the bottom) and back.
- [ ] Turn on Greek/Hebrew at the bottom: words appear under each verse.
- [ ] Turn the phone sideways: the text and bars fit, nothing is cut off, and nothing
      lies under the phone's own buttons or camera at the side. On the Malayalam and
      Tamil apps the two Bibles stand side by side, the one that was open on the left.
- [ ] With English open, turn the phone sideways: English is on the left. Tap the
      speaker: it reads English. Turn the phone back: English is still open.

- [ ] Books tab: all 66 books at once, Old Testament down the left and New Testament down
      the right, names only, the book being read marked and in view.
      Old Testament or New Testament above shows that Testament alone in one list; All
      brings both columns back. Long names (Song of Solomon, 1 Thessalonians, and the
      Malayalam and Tamil names) take two lines and are not cut.
- [ ] Books tab after reading a few chapters: a clock at the right of the title opens
      the chapters read lately (not the one open now); one tap opens that chapter. On a
      fresh install, with nothing read yet, the clock is not there.
- [ ] Four-language app, first opening: the language screen shows the phone's language
      first, ticked, with Continue in that language; a tap opens that Bible. Set the phone
      to Hindi and reinstall: हिन्दी stands first and opens the Hindi Bible with Hindi menus
      and a one-time card (समझ गया) saying the Hindi words are not linked yet. Every screen
      (Settings, How to use, Why this Bible, Privacy, the notebook) reads in Hindi.
- [ ] Four-language app, reader: the Bible pill (हिन्दी, മലയാളം, KJV…) opens the six
      Bibles under their own names; the swap button beside it returns to the Bible read
      before; in Search the row of Bibles under the box searches each, and a result opens
      in that Bible. Hindi footnote letters hang off the word before them.
- [ ] Search dictionary: with study tools on, two entries and a More row that opens the
      rest; with study tools off, no dictionary block.
- [ ] Tap the title in the reader: the picker opens on the book being read with its chapters;
      "All books" shows the Testament switch and the books under their groups. Pick 2 Kings
      5 and then Luke 15: each opens at once. A book read before opens on its grid with the
      last chapter marked.

## 3. Words and verses

- [ ] Tap an underlined word: the sheet shows the Greek or Hebrew word, its meaning
      in Malayalam (or English) and "See all verses". The speaker button says the word
      (it may need a Greek or Hebrew voice).
- [ ] Hold a verse: the menu opens. Try copy, share, bookmark, a highlight colour, a
      note, "Mark text" (drag over a few words), Compare.
- [ ] Add a verse to the notebook from that menu.
- [ ] Hold John 3:16 → Cross references: the list opens with the verses quoted; tap one
      and it opens, Back returns.
- [ ] Hold Matthew 8:28 → Parallel passages: Mark 5:1-3 and Luke 8:26-27, quoted. Also try
      1 Kings 10:1 (2 Chronicles 9) and Psalm 18:1 (2 Samuel 22).
- [ ] The "About …" line above a book's first chapter opens About this book for that book,
      in the app's language: who wrote it, when, the outline and key verses. Tap an outline
      section and a key verse: each opens at the right verse. The same page opens from "About
      this book" above the chapter numbers. Try Genesis, Psalms, Mark and
      Revelation. In the Malayalam and Tamil apps, switch the language to English and back.

## 4. Listen (read aloud)

- [ ] Tap the speaker in the top bar: the chapter is read in the phone's voice, the
      verse being read is shown, and it goes on to the next chapter.
- [ ] Pause, play, next and previous verse, speed.
- [ ] Lock the screen while it reads: note whether it carries on.
- [ ] Have someone call you while it reads: it stops or pauses, and you can carry on
      afterwards.
- [ ] Malayalam app: if the phone has no Malayalam voice, the message explains what to
      install.

## 5. Cards

- [ ] Hold a verse → Share as card. Pick an occasion, a design, square and tall.
- [ ] **Your photo**: the phone's own photo picker opens (the app asks for no photo
      permission). Pick a photo, crop it, try Light and Dark, Faint to Strong, Blur,
      Top/Middle/Bottom, Book/Plain, Pictures and lines.
- [ ] Share picture to WhatsApp (or another app): the picture is sharp and looks like
      the preview.
- [ ] Save as my design. Close the app completely (swipe it away), open it again,
      open a card: the saved design is still first in the designs, with its photo.
- [ ] Long-press the saved design: it can be removed.

## 6. Notebook, Saved, Search

- [ ] New page in the notebook: type a title and "John 3:16" (or യോഹന്നാൻ 3:16); after
      Done the reference is a link and the verse is quoted under it. Edit and share
      the page; delete a page.
- [ ] **Handwritten page**: New page → Handwritten, lined paper. Write a few lines with
      a finger: the line follows the finger without lag. Try each pen, Thick, the
      highlighter over writing (the writing stays dark), the eraser, Undo and Redo.
- [ ] Move the page with two fingers: it moves and nothing is written. Write near the
      foot: the page grows.
- [ ] **With a pen** (S Pen, Apple Pencil, any active stylus): write with the pen while
      the hand rests on the screen. After the first pen stroke the app says the pen was
      found; the resting hand writes nothing, and one finger now moves the page.
      Note the phone or tablet and the pen.
- [ ] Type a title, leave the page, close the app completely, open the page again: the
      writing is all there. Switch to plain paper and back. Share the page: the picture
      shows the title and the writing.
- [ ] A handwritten page is not offered in "Add to notebook" from the verse menu.
- [ ] On a handwritten page, the pen-only switch (stylus / hand icon) turns pen-only on and
      off, and the line under the tools says how the page moves. Share a page as a picture
      in dark mode: the picture is light paper with dark writing.
- [ ] Write a long handwritten page (ten screens or more): writing stays smooth to the end
      [device: Android especially]. Its PDF opens and has the writing on the right sheets.
- [ ] Share a typed page as PDF and a handwritten page as PDF: the PDF opens with the
      title, the quoted verses (typed) or the writing on its paper, a long page over
      several A4 pages.
- [ ] Notebook → Back up or restore → Save a backup: save the file to Drive or Files.
      Delete one page, change another, then Restore from a backup and pick the file: the
      deleted page is back, the changed page keeps the newer change, a handwritten page
      has its writing. Picking a photo or another file says it is not a backup.
- [ ] Restore, in the Malayalam app, a backup made in the English app: the app asks first.
- [ ] Settings → Privacy shows the policy in the app's language.
- [ ] At the largest text size (36) About this book and Why this Bible are at that size, and
      the backup sheet shows its whole explanation ("Nothing is deleted").
- [ ] Saved shows the bookmarks, highlights and notes from section 3.
- [ ] Search for a word: results start in the New Testament; the Old and New Testament
      and book buttons narrow the list; Show more adds verses. Search for a reference
      (John 3:16) and a number (G26).

## 7. Settings and display

- [ ] Settings → How to use: each topic opens and closes; the text is in the app's
      language and follows the text size. The reader's first tip has a How to use link.
- [ ] Settings → Study tools off: words lose their underlines and do nothing when tapped,
      the Hebrew/Greek pill is gone from the bottom bar, the verse menu has no Compare,
      Cross references, Parallel passages or Words in this verse, and the chapter list has
      no About this book row. Tap the graduation cap in the reader's top bar: everything
      comes back, the cap fills in, and a note says Study tools on; tap it again to go plain.
- [ ] Settings → Write to us opens the mail app with the address, a subject naming the
      app and version, and a last line naming the build and the phone. On a phone with no
      mail app, a message shows the address with Copy address. The invitation card above
      the chapter appears about two weeks after install (to try it sooner, set the phone's
      date forward and reopen the app): Not now hides it, No thanks hides it for good,
      Write to us opens the mail and hides it for good.

- [ ] Each theme (Light, Sepia, Dark, follow the phone); Fluorescent highlights in dark.
- [ ] **With the app open on a chapter**, switch the phone's own dark mode on and off
      (quick settings): the whole screen changes, the title included.
- [ ] Largest text size in the app, and the phone's largest font (Android Settings →
      Display → Font size): nothing overlaps or is cut off in the reader, the word
      sheet, the verse menu and Settings.
- [ ] Interface language English/Malayalam (or Tamil) switches every screen.

## 8. Accessibility

- [ ] Turn on TalkBack (Android Settings → Accessibility). Every button says what it
      does (in the interface language), a verse can be read, a tab can be changed.

## 9. Tablet and Chromebook

- [ ] A tablet on its side: tapping a word opens the panel at the right and the text
      stays in view; another word can be tapped with the panel open.
- [ ] Chromebook keyboard: ← and → turn the chapter, Ctrl+F opens search, Esc closes a
      sheet or goes back.

## 10. Odd cases

- [ ] Phone nearly full (under 500 MB free): the app still opens; a fresh install says
      if there is not room to prepare the text.
- [ ] Leave the app for a few hours (or open many other apps), come back: it opens at
      the same place.
- [ ] Back button / back gesture closes a sheet first, then goes back a screen, and
      leaves the app from the reader.

When something is wrong, send the notes and screenshots; say what you did just
before it happened.
