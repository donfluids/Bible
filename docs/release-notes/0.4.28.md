# 0.4.28 (build 55)

Released 2 November 2026 for all four apps: Bible, വേദപുസ്തകം, வேதாகமம் and Bibles.
These notes cover the changes since 0.4.27 (build 54). The APKs and bundles are on the
`apk-builds` branch.

## Verse cards

- Six new designs without pictures: Linen, Gold, Dawn, Olive, Night and Ink. They join
  Paper at the front of the design row and use colour, lines, the cross and stars only.
  The designs with pictures follow them.
- On a phone, the card stays at the top while the choices scroll under it, so each
  change shows at once.
- The verse row says "Verse 3" or "Verses 3–5".
- Text position, typeface, pictures and lines, and Save as my design are under More
  options.
- Occasions wrap onto a second line instead of running off the screen.
- The empty heading box shows a hint, and the Tall (status) size is now called Status.

## Typed notes

- While you write, the last verse reference on your line appears above the keyboard
  with its text. Insert text puts the verse under the line.
- Lists carry on. Press return after "- " or "1. " and the next line starts the same
  way. Return on an empty item ends the list.
- With more than two pages, the notebook has a search box that looks through titles
  and writing.
- The note box for a verse shows the verse above it and has more room.

## Handwritten notes

- Pinch with two fingers to make the page up to three times larger. Move it in any
  direction with two fingers. Tap the zoom label to fit the page again.
- A shared picture of the page is always the normal size.
- The notebook list shows the top of each handwritten page, so pages can be told apart.

## Read aloud

- The voice list in Settings is grouped by accent: India first, then Britain, the United
  States, Australia and any others.
- Voices are numbered within each group, and the engine's codes are no longer shown.
- The phone's own voices come before online ones.

## Reading

- A single psalm is written "Psalm 23:1" everywhere a reference appears.

## Behind the scenes

- iPhone versions of the three single-language apps now compile on GitHub's Mac
  machines. They are not signed and cannot be installed yet.

## Google Play "What's new" (500 characters at most)

characters, the Malayalam 331 and the Tamil 364. The Malayalam and Tamil texts need a Malayalam and a Tamil reader before upload.

```
• Lists continue on the next line, and pages can be searched

• Read aloud: voices grouped by accent, India first

```
