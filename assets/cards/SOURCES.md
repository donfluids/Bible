# Verse card artwork

- `birthday_cake.png`, `balloon.png`, `party_popper.png`, `wrapped_gift.png`,
  `christmas_tree.png`, `ring.png`, `bouquet.png`, `dove.png`, `tulip.png`,
  `two_hearts.png`: Microsoft Fluent Emoji, 3D style
  (https://github.com/microsoft/fluentui-emoji), MIT License, Copyright (c) Microsoft
  Corporation. Unchanged.
- `redoute_rose.png`: Pierre-Joseph Redouté, "Empress Josephine" or Frankfort Rose
  (Rosa turbinata), from *Les Roses* (1817–24). The Metropolitan Museum of Art, object
  762055, Open Access (public domain, CC0). The paper is keyed out, the caption cropped
  and the edges faded by `scripts/card-rose.mjs`.
