# Verse card artwork

Old prints and paintings, all public domain. Each is cropped to its subject, with the
paper keyed out and the edges faded, by `scripts/card-art.mjs` (the rose by
`scripts/card-rose.mjs`). The crop of each, in fractions of the museum's image, is the
third argument; the last argument, where given, is a box of lettering to take out.

- `lawrance_wreath.png`: Mary Lawrance, "A Wreath of Roses", 1799. Cleveland Museum of
  Art 1941.387, CC0. Crop 0.125,0.05,0.93,0.95; the word "Frontispiece" in the ring taken
  out (0.24,0.3,0.76,0.7).
- `monnoyer_bouquet.png`: Jean-Baptiste Monnoyer, "Bouquet", c. 1680–95. Cleveland Museum
  of Art 1955.466, CC0. Crop 0.15,0.08,0.92,0.86.
- `piquet_lily.png`: Auguste Piquet de Brienne, "Witte lelie" (white lily), 1822.
  Rijksmuseum RP-P-1922-355, Public Domain Mark. Crop 0.2,0.12,0.85,0.78.
- `audubon_doves.png`: Robert Havell after John James Audubon, "Carolina Pigeon or Turtle
  Dove", 1827. National Gallery of Art, Washington 1945.8.17, CC0. Crop 0.25,0.03,0.85,0.4.
- `holsteyn_tulip.png`: Pieter Holsteyn II, "Study of a Tulip (Ammirael Winckel)",
  c. 1645. Cleveland Museum of Art 2019.4, CC0. Crop 0.12,0.025,0.9,0.9.
- `prang_holly.png`: Louis Prang & Co., "Holly", from "Autumn Leaves", 1862. The
  Metropolitan Museum of Art 48.61(86), CC0. Crop 0.15,0.1,0.85,0.78.
- `hoefnagel_dove.png`: Joris Hoefnagel, Plate 66: Doves and Pigeons, c. 1575/1590s,
  one white dove. National Gallery of Art, Washington 1987.20.8.67, CC0. Crop
  0.47,0.16,0.72,0.35.
- `redoute_rose.png`: Pierre-Joseph Redouté, "Empress Josephine" or Frankfort Rose
  (Rosa turbinata), from *Les Roses* (1817–24). The Metropolitan Museum of Art, object
  762055, Open Access (public domain, CC0). The paper is keyed out, the caption cropped
  and the edges faded by `scripts/card-rose.mjs`.
